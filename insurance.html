OEM or aftermarket?

Glass type describes the replacement part. A repair treats eligible damage in the existing windshield. A replacement installs a new pane. Ask the shop to confirm which service is suitable.

OEM glass

A replacement part supplied through the vehicle maker’s original equipment program. Confirm fit and features using the VIN.
